Article 2 (Purpose of the Service)

The App is a tool that lets users simply manage the tasks they need to do "today" and start each day fresh. Tasks can be organized into lists, and you can add repeating tasks and tasks with a due date. The App can also send a notification (a reminder) on the days and at the time you choose, to help you start your day (off by default; used only when you set it up). If you wish, you can also use iCloud to sync your data and carry it over to other devices signed in with the same Apple ID (sync; off by default, and used only when you turn it on). So that users are not weighed down by yesterday's unfinished tasks, pages of past days that have passed the reset time cannot be viewed on screen. Daily records are deleted automatically from your device once they are about one year old.
