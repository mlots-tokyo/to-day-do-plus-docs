Privacy Policy

Mlots (representative: Chikaarashi Rikito) ("we," "us") sets out this Privacy Policy (the "Policy") on how we handle user information in the smartphone application "To Day Do Plus" (the "App").

Article 1 (Information We Collect and How We Use It)

The App is designed with privacy in mind. We do not directly collect users' personal information, send it to a server, or store it.

1. Data stored by the App

  • What: The names of tasks you enter, their completion status and order, list names, repeat settings, due dates and start dates, daily records (the tasks that appeared on each day and whether they were completed), settings such as the reset time and reminder notifications (on/off, notification time, notification days), whether the tutorial has been shown, and the number of times you have completed all your tasks.
  • Purpose of use: To show tasks in the App, to create pages for repeating tasks and for each day, to switch days automatically, and to help you stay motivated.
  • Handling: This data is stored on your device (AsyncStorage). Daily records are deleted automatically from your device once they are about one year old. If you turn on the sync feature (Article 5), the data is also stored in your own Apple account's iCloud, and can be carried over to other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. We do not view or collect this data.

Article 2 (External APIs and Communication)

The App does not use external APIs to obtain user information, and does not send data to any server of our own. It does not use advertising or usage analytics tools. Task management is designed to work fully offline. Only when you turn on sync (iCloud) from the menu is your data saved, through Apple's iCloud, in your own iCloud. This does not pass through our servers, and we do not receive the data (Article 5).

Article 3 (Purchase)

The App is provided as a paid download on the App Store (a one-time purchase). Payment is handled through Apple's services; Apple handles your payment information, and we do not receive it. How information is handled during purchase follows Apple's privacy policy.

Article 4 (Reminder Notifications)

Only when you set it up, the App shows a notice (a notification) prompting you to open it, on the days and at the time you choose. The notification is scheduled and shown inside your own device (a local notification), and its text is fixed (it does not include the content of your tasks). It does not pass through our servers, and we do not receive information that identifies your device for this purpose. Notifications are off by default; they are shown only after you turn them on from the menu and allow them on the iPhone's confirmation screen. You can stop them at any time by turning them off, or by not allowing notifications in the iPhone's Settings.

Article 5 (Data Sync (iCloud))

The App has a feature (sync) that syncs your data to iCloud and carries it over to other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Sync is off by default and is used only when you turn it on from the menu.

  • Data that is synced: The data listed in Article 1 (tasks, lists, repeat settings, due dates and start dates, daily records, settings such as the reset time and reminder notifications, and so on).
  • Where it is stored: In your own account, in iCloud provided by Apple. We do not use our own servers, and we do not view or collect the synced data.
  • Handling: Storage and transfer of data in iCloud follow Apple's terms of service and privacy policy. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or your storage is full, data is not synced and is stored only on your device.
  • How to stop it: You can turn sync on or off in the menu. Turning it off does not automatically delete data already saved in iCloud (Article 8).

Article 6 (Request to Rate the App)

When certain conditions are met, the App may show the iPhone's standard screen asking you to rate it on the App Store. Apple handles what you enter on that screen, and we do not receive it in a form that can identify you.

Article 7 (Provision to Third Parties and External Transmission)

We do not provide data handled in the App to third parties, or send it outside, without your consent. Saving data to your own iCloud when you turn on sync is based on your own action and does not fall under this.

Article 8 (Deleting Data)

Data on your device is erased when you delete the App from your device. We cannot restore erased data. Daily records are also deleted automatically from your device once they are about one year old (if sync is on, older records may remain on the iCloud side). If sync was on, data saved in iCloud remains even after you delete the App, and may become available again on another device or when you reinstall the App. If you want to delete the data saved in iCloud, delete this App's data stored in iCloud from the iPhone's Settings. Also, your device's backups (iCloud and so on) may include the App's data. How backups are handled follows the settings and terms of Apple's services.

Article 9 (Compliance with Laws and Review of This Policy)

We comply with applicable Japanese laws and other norms, and review this Policy as appropriate to improve it. This Policy was revised to reflect the addition of the feature that syncs data to iCloud and the App being offered as a paid app. If we change how data is handled in the future, we will revise this Policy beforehand and let you know.

Article 10 (Disclaimer)

If you are taken from the App to an external site (such as a store review page) through a link or the like, we are not responsible for the information or services provided on the destination site.
